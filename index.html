Project Idea

This Assignment Tracker web application will be used to help students organize their assignments, deadlines, and all of their upcoming schoolwork in one place.

Possible Features

  • Add and track your assignments
  • Set due dates
  • Mark assignments as completed
  • View upcoming assignments
  • Prioritize assignments based on due date
